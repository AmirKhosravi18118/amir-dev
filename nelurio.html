How it works

From visitor to user in three steps.

No installation, no credit card — pick an app and start.

1

Pick an app

Every card above is live. Choose the tool that fits what you need today.

2

Create your account

Sign up inside the app with email — or with one click via Google Sign-in where it's offered. Your account keeps your work safe across devices.

3

Your data stays yours

EU-hosted infrastructure, GDPR-first defaults — and you can delete your account and all its data yourself, any time.
