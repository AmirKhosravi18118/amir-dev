When you visit this website, the hoster processes technically necessary data in server log files (shortened IP address, time, requested address, browser type). The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (secure and efficient operation). Log files are deleted after 14 days.

Reach measurement without cookies

This website uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. For anonymous, cookieless counting of clicks on contact actions, your browser sends a small event signal (event name, time, page) to /api/event on this server. No personal profiles are built and no data is shared with third parties. The signal is not sent automatically when your browser reports "Do Not Track".
