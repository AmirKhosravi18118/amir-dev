Finello

Students in Germany need a simple overview of budget, recurring payments and spending.

View live Open gallery Request similar

The project

Finello is a finance app for students in Germany. It gives one clear view of the monthly budget, recurring payments and spending, so rent, semester ticket and everyday costs are no surprise.

What it does

  • Plans the month in a budget calendar
  • Tracks recurring payments such as rent and subscriptions
  • Records expenses and shows where the money goes
  • Analyses spending by category and over time

How it looks

A clean, light interface with a deep green summary card on top, clear numbers and a calendar that shows every payment on its day.

Solution
A finance app with a budget calendar, recurring payments and an expense overview.
Tech
  • React 19
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind 4
