Haustechnik Monfared

The project

Haustechnik Monfared is a heating, plumbing and air-conditioning company in Wiesbaden. I rebuilt its old WordPress site as a modern platform: a bilingual website where customers request appointments, and a staff area for the team.

What it does

  • Presents services and products and takes appointment requests
  • Works in German and English
  • Gives each employee their own login with roles and permissions
  • Shows jobs, tasks and the team in one panel

How it looks

The website is bright and trustworthy: a strong blue hero with a portrait of the master craftsman, key facts underneath and orange action buttons. The panel is clean and light, with a menu on the left and clear job cards.

Solution
Rebuilt as a business platform: a bilingual website with appointment requests and a staff area with roles, permissions and an audit log.
Tech
  • Next.js 15
  • TypeScript
  • DE / EN
  • Role-based access
