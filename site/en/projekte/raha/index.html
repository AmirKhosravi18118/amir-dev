RahaVelvet

An artist sells artworks to customers in Europe and Iran and needs a store she can run herself.

Open gallery Request similar

The project

RahaVelvet is a store for an artist who sells original artworks. The design shows a calm, gallery-like site with a collection overview, detailed artwork pages and an order form. The artist will manage works, prices and availability herself in her own admin area. The project is in development.

What it does

  • Shows the collection as a clean grid
  • Gives every artwork its own page with details
  • Takes orders through a simple form
  • Lets the artist manage works, prices and availability herself
  • Prices in euro, interface in Persian and English

How it looks

A warm cream background, large serif headlines and artworks presented like pieces on a gallery wall, with a deep wine red for actions.

Solution
A luxury storefront in Persian and English with euro prices and an admin area where the artist manages works and prices herself.
