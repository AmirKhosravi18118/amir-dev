SAAD Tattoo

A tattoo studio in Wiesbaden needed a site that shows its work and brings in appointment requests.

View live Open gallery Request similar

The project

SAAD Tattoo is a tattoo studio in Wiesbaden. The website shows the studio’s work, explains each tattoo category and lets visitors book step by step: category, service, date, time, confirmation. It also presents the studio’s training courses.

What it does

  • Shows the work in a large gallery
  • Explains each category on its own service page
  • Guides visitors through a multi-step booking
  • Presents training courses for tattoo artists
  • Speaks German, English and Persian

How it looks

Black and matte gold, huge outlined headlines and full-width photography. Calm, bold and made for people who already know what they want.

Solution
Redesign in black and gold with a gallery, a multi-step booking flow, training pages and three languages: German, English, Persian.
Tech
  • Vanilla JS
  • DE / EN / FA
  • RTL
  • Booking flow
