Washhalle

Services, prices and the monthly report of a car wash needed to be recorded and checked in one place.

View live Open gallery Request similar

The project

Washhalle is an operations app for a car-wash business. The team records every service per shift, the app checks prices against the price rules and flags anything that needs a second look. The owner gets daily and monthly overviews.

What it does

  • Records services per vehicle and shift
  • Checks prices against yearly price rules
  • Flags entries for a double check
  • Handles approval requests and alerts
  • Shows revenue and team performance

How it looks

Fresh green on white: a dashboard with big numbers, weekly charts and clear tables, made for quick use on a phone between two cars.

Solution
Operations app for shifts: record services, check prices automatically, flag double-checks and generate the monthly report.
